# PCA Summary

## Objective
Reduce the processed employee features to two principal components
to visualize workforce segments.

## Results
- Employee records: 5000
- Processed features/records: 7487
- Principal components: 2
- PC1 variance explained: 8.38%
- PC2 variance explained: 5.98%
- Combined variance explained: 14.36%

## Interpretation
The PCA plot projects employees onto two dimensions.
The percentage above indicates how much of the processed data's
variation is represented by these two components. Some information
is lost when the original feature space is reduced to two dimensions.
